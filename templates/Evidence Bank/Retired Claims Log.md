# Retired Claims Log

Wording and figures that never go back on paper. Append, never delete. The pre-send gate reads every phrase in "quotes" in the "Retired wording" column (18 characters or longer) and flags it on any page, including inside approved bullets. Quotes in the other columns are ignored. Replace the example row with your first real one. Put shorter retired phrases in settings/resume.json under RETIRED_PHRASES, so they get checked too.
