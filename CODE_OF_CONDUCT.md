# Code of conduct

This is a small project for people who are looking for a job. That can be a
stressful time, so the bar here is simple.

## What's expected
- Be kind and patient. Assume the other person is doing their best.
- Keep feedback about the kit, never about the person.
- Respect privacy. Never post anyone's resume, job postings they saved,
  tracker data, or contact details, including your own.

## What isn't okay
- Insults, harassment, or unwelcome personal attention of any kind.
- Remarks that put someone down for who they are or for their job situation.
- Sharing someone's private information.
- Spam or recruiting pitches in issues and pull requests.

## Where this applies
Issues, pull requests, discussions, and anywhere else this project is the
topic.

## Reporting a problem
Report it privately: on this repository's **Security** tab, choose **Report a
vulnerability** and say it's a conduct report. Only the maintainer sees it.
You can also use GitHub's own **Report content** link on any comment.

## What happens next
The maintainer reads every report and replies within a few days. Depending on
what happened, the result may be a private note, a removed comment, or a block
from the project. Reports stay private.
